Our Story

Peak Performance Fitness began with three college friends and one shared frustration: finding a gym that felt affordable, welcoming, and easy to fit into a busy schedule. Between classes and workouts, Grace, Andrew, and Jaxson imagined a place where beginners could ask questions and experienced athletes could keep challenging themselves.

They turned that idea into a neighborhood gym with flexible memberships, motivating group classes, and personal training. Today, their goal is the same: help every member feel comfortable showing up and proud of the progress they make.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need gym experience to join?

No! Every starting point belongs here. Our team can show you around, and personal training is available if you would like individual guidance.

Can I try the gym before buying a membership?

Yes. Our $12 Day Pass gives you access to cardio and strength equipment for one day during staffed hours.

Is there a student discount?

Our Student Membership is $24 per month with a valid student ID. It includes the Basic Membership benefits.

Are group classes included?

Unlimited group classes are included with Peak Plus at $49 per month. Individual classes are $10 each, with no membership required.

Can I bring a workout buddy?

Peak Plus includes one guest pass each month. Your buddy can also purchase a Day Pass for a visit during staffed hours.

Does walking in count as my first rep?

We will count it as your first step! Bring comfortable clothes, water, and a willingness to start. Matching workout outfits are completely optional.
